# à la jam

a band that plays with you.

you sit at a hand-drawn piano with a jar of jam on it. play a few notes, the jar tips, and the band comes in. the keys light up with the notes that sound good over the chords, so anything lit is yours to play. the band keeps the groove, you jam on top.

**play it here:** https://blip-dotcom-prog.github.io/a-la_jam/

## how to play

any browser works, on a computer, a tablet or a phone. turn the sound up.

on a computer, click the drawn keys or play your computer keys (G is middle C). a USB or MIDI keyboard is a bonus, not a must: plug it in and tap the little keyboard at the top (Chrome, Edge or Firefox).

on an iPhone or iPad, Safari asks you to keep it on your home screen first: tap share, then add to home screen, then open à la jam from there. it plays full screen, like an app. a phone gets its own pocket version.
